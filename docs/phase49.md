# Phase 49 - campaign hero units

## What changed
Campaign missions now spawn one player hero unit at the start.

- Hero uses the player's faction unique unit (Thumper, Mantis or Skyfall).
- Hero gets +35% max health and +25% damage.
- Hero is marked with a `HERO` prefix in the selection panel.
- Mission briefing shows the hero's name.
- Skirmish missions do not spawn a hero.

## Verification

- EditMode **90/90**
- PlayMode **77/77**
- Windows player built.

## Known limits
- Hero is not persistent across missions yet.
- Hero resets to full health each mission.
- No hero progression, abilities or unique portrait.
- No branching missions.
